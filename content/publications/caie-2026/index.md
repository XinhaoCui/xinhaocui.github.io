## Overview

The location of a maritime support center shapes the routes available to unmanned surface vessels and aerial vehicles. The upper level chooses the center location. The lower level coordinates routes for both vehicle types under uncertain demand and navigation conditions. A two-stage solution method combines constrained clustering with hybrid evolutionary search.

## Findings

Across the scenarios tested in the South China Sea case study, plans from the bilevel model cost 0.59–12.20% less than plans from the comparison methods.
