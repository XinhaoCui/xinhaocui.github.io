## Overview

The study addresses how to allocate limited intervention resources as a social network changes. Sensor data update a digital twin of the network. A mixed-integer model uses that representation to recommend interventions, which domain experts can review and adjust.

## Findings

The deployment in Guiyang used monitoring data from 2,416 registered individuals. In the study’s comparison with conventional targeting, adaptive population segmentation improved the harm-reduction measure by 3.9%.
