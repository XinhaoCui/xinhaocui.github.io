## Overview

The study models repair missions in which restoring one unit can depend on the recovery of others. The model matches crews to tasks by skill and plans their routes. Its objective considers both restoration speed and mission reliability. The matheuristic repeatedly reoptimizes selected portions of the schedule.

## Findings

The method found the best solutions among the approaches compared on simulated instances that varied in size and in the density of recovery dependencies.
